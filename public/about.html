Introduction

Welcome to my personal website — a quiet, digital space where I share a bit of who I am. This site is a small window into my everyday life: the things I love, the things I'm learning, and the things I'm building as I grow.

I'm Christian Corpez — known simply as Chris — a 1st Year BS Agriculture student majoring in Agricultural Extension at Eastern Visayas State University – Burauen Campus. I'm also a coffee enthusiast, a morning runner, and someone who plays guitar when the evening gets quiet.

This website is my own way of collecting those pieces — a place to look back on moments, track what I'm learning, and share what matters to me. Simple, honest, and made with care.
